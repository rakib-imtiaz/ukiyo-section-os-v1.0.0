Our recommendations

What we should do, in order

Eight actions. NOW = start immediately · NEXT = next 3–5 months · LATER = after the SaaS launches.

1
Prove it on one store first
Finish ECO ELI end-to-end with a passing match score; record real AI cost, fix rounds and hours. Nothing is sold before this.
NOWImpact: unlocks everything· weeks 0–2
2
Launch the done-for-you service
Convert clients’ sites at ~$2,000 a store (far below agency rates). Register as a Shopify Partner and refer every store.
NOWImpact: ~$12k/mo + referral income· from week 2
3
Add an ownership check
Customers confirm they own the site or have the rights to it. Get a short legal review of copying websites.
NOWImpact: removes copyright risk· before first client
4
Cut the AI cost per store
Sonnet 5.5 by default, cheap models for planning and summaries, overnight batch builds, a cost cap per build.
NEXTImpact: $45 → ~$11–21· weeks 4–10
5
Move the SaaS engine to Claude’s API
Anthropic doesn’t allow reselling Claude usage through Claude Code. Selling credits needs our own agent on the API (plus OpenRouter).
NEXTImpact: makes credits legal· weeks 4–10
6
Launch the agency licence
$3,995 once + $1,195/yr. Agencies use their own Claude login or API key through Claude Code — allowed today, zero AI cost for us.
NEXTImpact: 10 licences ≈ $40k yr 1· weeks 8–14
7
Launch SaaS plans with credits
Freelancer $99, Studio $349, Agency $999. Cap builds and fix rounds; keep checks and previews free; top-ups at 2× cost.
NEXTImpact: ~$16k/mo by month 12· weeks 12–20
8
List on the Shopify App Store
Billing through Shopify (0% on the first $1M). Keep Stripe for agencies on development stores. Apply to OpenAI’s plan-sharing programme.
LATERImpact: discovery + trust· after week 20
✕ Don’t do

Don’t mass-produce themes for the Theme Store — Horizon-based or look-alike themes are rejected. At most 1–2 original themes on Skeleton, later.

Don’t sell "unlimited" plans — AI cost makes them lose money (Replit, Windsurf, Cursor all had to switch to usage pricing).

Don’t build our own "Sign in with Claude" or store customers’ Claude logins — Anthropic forbids it.

Don’t resell Claude usage while running Claude Code — credits only on our own API agent.

Don’t price close to cost — every build and fix round at about 2× what it costs us.

Bottom line: earn now with the service, use that time to make the engine cheaper and legal for credits, then sell the tool two ways — credits for small teams, a licence for agencies.
