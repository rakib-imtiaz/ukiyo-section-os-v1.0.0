5 · What a rush really saves

Where speed helps, and where it doesn't

AI writes code fast. Most of ThemeBot's time goes to testing on real stores, and more hours don't make that part faster.

Desktop v1 in 2 weeksIf a store passes the check by day 4
Rush works
Web app first version in 4 weeksFor a few beta users; expect about a week of fixes after
Works, with fixes after
Lower AI cost & creditsNeeds many real test builds, and each one takes hours
Rush doesn't help
Signed installer, Mac, App StoreWaiting on Microsoft, Apple and Shopify
Rush doesn't help
Problems we only found by testing on real stores
Wrong store pickedSetup chose another account's store
Surprise Shopify pop-upA new screen stopped the setup
Build stopped halfwayA restart cut a long build short
False "limit reached"A warning showed when nothing was wrong
Old details rememberedStore data from a previous account

Each one was quick to fix once we saw it, but we only saw it by running real builds. A rush runs into the same problems with less time to fix them, and sometimes in front of customers.

If we rush everything
First agencies meet the bugs
Time moves from building to fixing
Harder to win trust back
If we rush only desktop v1
Something real to sell in 2 weeks
Real feedback guides the web app
Each release works the first time
Suggested approach: move fast on desktop v1, then keep a steady pace. The first agencies will remember whether it worked more than how fast it arrived.

10 · "AI writes the code, so why does it take time?"

Why not everything in 2 weeks?

AI writes code in days. The time goes to proving it works, waiting on other companies, and letting customers try it.

Builds take hoursThe AI builds a store in hours, not seconds. Testing 10 stores takes days.
It must pass the checkNo store has fully passed yet, and we don't know how many fix rounds it takes.
Shopify sets its own paceApp Store review and approvals take weeks. We can't speed them up.
Certificates & paymentsThe installer certificate and payment account need outside approval.
One personYou build, sell, support and deliver client stores. Each one takes time.
Taking money safelyBilling and online building must be tested before strangers pay.
Short answer: AI makes the coding fast, which is why desktop v1 sells in 2 weeks. On a 60-hour week, both first versions arrive in about 4 weeks and everything in about 11. Testing, Shopify's review and customer feedback don't speed up with AI.
