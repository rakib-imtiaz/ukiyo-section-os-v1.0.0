ThemeBot · how it works

From a website to a working Shopify store, step by step

Every box below is something you will see on screen. Amber boxes are the moments where you do something. Teal boxes are ThemeBot working on its own, inside your Chrome.

1 · The big picture

What goes in, what comes out

Two things go in. One store comes out. Everything in between is automatic, and it happens in your own Chrome.

3 · "Do it for me", start to finish

The full path, with every place it can pause

Read it top to bottom. There are exactly three things you do: fill in the form, say "yes, that's my website", and confirm your Chrome once. Everything else is ThemeBot, working inside your own Chrome where you are already signed in to Shopify.

ThemeBot never types card or payment details. If Shopify will not continue without a card, the run stops and says so. Nothing is charged, nothing is half-built.
4 · Build, check, fix

How it makes sure the copy matches

Building the theme is only the first pass. After that it compares the new store to your website page by page, fixes what differs, and goes again, until the match is good enough or it runs out of rounds.

%%{init: {"theme":"base","themeVariables":{"fontFamily":"Source Sans 3, Segoe UI, sans-serif","fontSize":"14px","lineColor":"#4A5568"}}}%%
flowchart TD
  B1["Rebuild the website
as Shopify sections"]:::bot --> B2["Put it on the store
as a draft theme"]:::bot
  B2 --> Q1["Open both sites
and compare every page:
layout · text · clickable things"]:::bot
  Q1 --> M{"Match good enough?
(you set the bar, default 92%)"}:::ask
  M -- "Yes" --> OK["Finished · draft theme ready
your live store untouched"]:::done
  M -- "Not yet" --> F["Fix the differences"]:::bot
  F --> N{"Rounds left?
(default: up to 5)"}:::ask
  N -- "Yes" --> Q1
  N -- "No" --> RV["Needs a look · best version kept,
list of what still differs"]:::pause

Quiet automatic checkThe default. A hidden browser does the comparing in the background. Nothing to install or sign in to.
Check in my Chrome with ClaudeOptional. Claude opens the pages in your own Chrome and checks them the way a person would. One three-minute setup, remembered forever.
Publish live?Off by default. The result stays a draft until you look at it. Turn it on and a passing build replaces the live theme automatically.
5 · What you see while it runs

Seven lines, ticking through

While ThemeBot works, the screen shows this list. Each line turns green as it finishes. If one turns red, the message under it says what happened and what to do.

1. Check that your Chrome is readyClaude extension, signed in to Shopify.
2. Create your Shopify storeNo card needed.
3. Set up the connectionBetween ThemeBot and the store.
4. Save the connection keysTo your project, encrypted.
5. Test that ThemeBot can edit the store
6. Get your website ready to compare against
7. Start building your Shopify themeThen follow it on the Dashboard.
