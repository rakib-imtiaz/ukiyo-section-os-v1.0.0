ThemeBot

Your website, turned into a real Shopify store — automatically.

In one sentence: you give ThemeBot a website and a store name. It creates the Shopify store, connects to it, rebuilds the website as a proper Shopify theme, then checks and fixes its own work until the two match.
1 Tell it about the store

Store name, website link, and/or the website's files.

2 Confirm the website

ThemeBot shows you a picture of the site. You say "yes, that's the one".

3 Sign in once

One Shopify sign-in. Nothing starts before that.

4 ThemeBot sets up the store

Creates the store (no card), connects to it, runs a test edit.

5 Build, check, fix

Rebuilds the site as a theme, compares, fixes, reports.

1 The control room

Home screen

One place to start a build, see every project, follow progress, and watch ThemeBot work in its built-in browser.

2 Choose how to start

New build

"Do it for me" if there is no Shopify store yet. "I already have a store" if one was set up by hand. Same result either way.

3 Tell it about the store

Step 1 of 3

A name, an optional link, and a drop zone for the website's files. Three things, then it is automatic.

4 "Is this your website?"

Step 2 of 3

ThemeBot opens the site (or runs the uploaded files) and shows a picture of the home page before anything is created. One click to confirm.

5 Ready to go

Step 3 of 3

Sign in once, then press Start

  • The sign-in check. ThemeBot looks at whether its browser is signed in to Shopify. If not, it opens the sign-in page and waits. The Start button stays locked until you are in.
  • How it checks its work. A quiet automatic check in the background (recommended), or a check inside your own Chrome with Claude.
  • What will happen. A plain list of every step ThemeBot is about to take, written for the person pressing the button.
ThemeBot never enters payment details. If Shopify insists on a card, the setup stops and says so.

6 Optional: check in your own Chrome

One-time setup

Three minutes, done once, remembered forever

  • Chrome and the Claude extension are detected automatically (the green ticks).
  • The three sign-ins are confirmed by you, once. Chrome keeps them; ThemeBot only remembers that the setup is complete.
  • Prefer not to bother? Pick the quiet automatic check instead and skip this entirely.
